# EU Grant Agreement (AGA V2.0) — Structural Spine

## Summary
The primary structural spine for the EU Grant Agreement under AGA V2.0, spanning introductory elements, core legal text (Chapters 1–6, Articles 1–44), and programmatic annexes (Annex 5)[cite: 1].

## Structural Breakdown

### 1. Introductory & Contextual Framework
- [[Introduction]] (pp. 10–16): Regulatory framework, grant types, corporate approach[cite: 1].
- [[Preamble]] (pp. 17–20): Contracting parties and contractual annexes[cite: 1].
- [[Data Sheet]] (pp. 21–28): Project data, grant amounts, funding rates, reporting, eligibility options[cite: 1].

### 2. Core Legal Text: Chapters 1–6
- [[Chapter 1 - General]] (pp. 29–31): Subject of the Agreement (Art 1), Definitions (Art 2)[cite: 1].
- [[Chapter 2 - Action]] (pp. 32–33): Action (Art 3), Duration and Starting Date (Art 4)[cite: 1].
- [[Chapter 3 - Grant]] (pp. 34–146): Grant (Art 5), Cost Eligibility (Art 6)[cite: 1].
- [[Chapter 4 - Grant Implementation]] (pp. 147–274): Consortium (Sec 1, Art 7–10), Action Execution (Sec 2, Art 11–18), Grant Administration (Sec 3, Art 19–26)[cite: 1].
- [[Chapter 5 - Consequences of Non-Compliance]] (pp. 275–318): Rejections/Reductions (Sec 1, Art 27–28), Suspension/Termination (Sec 2, Art 29–32), Damages/Sanctions (Sec 3, Art 33–34), Force Majeure (Sec 4, Art 35)[cite: 1].
- [[Chapter 6 - Final Provisions]] (pp. 319–346): Final Provisions (Art 36–44)[cite: 1].

### 3. Programmatic Annex
- [[Annex 5 - Specific Rules]] (pp. 347–430): Programmatic conditions (Confidentiality/Security, Ethics, Values, IPR, Communication, Procurement, Partnerships, ERC)[cite: 1].

## MGA Text vs Annotation Notes
The AGA structural hierarchy mirrors the EU Model Grant Agreement (MGA) layout, using grey text boxes for MGA provisions and annotated bold/underlined terms for detailed guidance[cite: 8].

# Introduction (pp. 10–16)

## Overview
The Introduction section outlines the legal and contextual framework of EU Grant Agreements, explaining their sui generis private law contractual nature and hierarchy under the EU Financial Regulation[cite: 2].

## Key Elements & Structural Features
- **EU Grants Regulatory Framework (p. 10):** Governs relationships between the EU Financial Regulation, EU Programme Regulations, and Model Grant Agreements (MGAs)[cite: 2].
- **Model Grant Agreement Focus (p. 10):** Focuses on the EU General MGA as the corporate baseline for program-specific MGAs[cite: 2].
- **Formatting Conventions (pp. 10–11):** 
  - Grey Text Boxes: MGA official text (bold/underlined terms annotated)[cite: 2].
  - Green Text: Examples; Red Text: Lists; Orange Text: Exceptions; Purple Text: Programme-specific rules[cite: 2].
- **Participant Roles Breakdown (pp. 12–16):** Defines operational and contractual distinctions for Coordinators, Beneficiaries, Affiliated Entities, Subcontractors, Suppliers, In-Kind Contributors, and FSTP Recipients[cite: 2].
- **Summary Matrix Table (p. 16):** Tabular mapping of participant types, execution rights, eligibility rules, and Annex 1 requirements[cite: 2].

# Preamble (pp. 17–20)

## Overview
The Preamble identifies the contracting parties (Granting Authority, Coordinator, Beneficiaries) and establishes the formal binding components and annexes of the Grant Agreement[cite: 2].

## Core Components
- **Contracting Parties (pp. 17–18):** Establishes the legal relations between the Granting Authority and the Consortium (or single mono-beneficiary)[cite: 2].
- **Signature Mechanics (p. 18):** Direct signature by Coordinator; accession via Annex 3 forms for other beneficiaries[cite: 2].
- **Binding Contract Components (pp. 17–18):**
  1. Preamble
  2. Terms and Conditions (including the Data Sheet)
  3. Annex 1 (Description of the Action)
  4. Annex 2 (Estimated Budget) & Annex 2a (Unit Costs)
  5. Annex 3 (Accession Forms) & Annex 3a (Joint Liability Declarations)
  6. Annex 4 (Model Financial Statements)
  7. Annex 5 (Specific Rules)[cite: 2].
- **Participant Register Data (p. 19):** Legal entity data auto-populated from the Portal Participant Register managed by LEARs[cite: 2].

# Data Sheet (pp. 21–28)

## Overview
The Data Sheet serves as the project-specific summary containing automated IT-generated options, grant parameters, reporting schedules, payment conditions, and applicable cost eligibility rules[cite: 2].

## Structure & Key Points
- **Function & Design (p. 21):** Automated IT label summaries; red brackets represent programme/action pre-selected options, green brackets indicate project-selected options[cite: 2].
- **Point 1 — General Data (p. 21):** Summary of call ID, topic, acronym, duration, starting date, and linked actions[cite: 2].
- **Point 2 — Participants (pp. 21–22):** Participant list table (COO, BEN, AE, AP roles, PIC, maximum grant amounts, entry/exit dates)[cite: 2].
- **Point 3 — Grant Options (pp. 22–23):** Maximum grant amount, funding rates, forms of funding, budget flexibility caps, and specific cost eligibility flags[cite: 2].
- **Point 4 — Reporting, Payments & Recoveries (pp. 24–27):** Schedules for continuous/periodic reporting, payment deadlines, pre-financing, Mutual Insurance Mechanism (MIM) retentions, CFS thresholds, and recovery liability rules[cite: 2].
- **Points 5 & 6 — Legal/Administrative Provisions (pp. 27–28):** Applicable law regimes, dispute forums, Annex 5 applicability flags, and document retention / audit time-limits (3 or 5 years)[cite: 2].

# Annotations & Formatting Conventions (pp. 10–11)

## Overview
Defines the visual, structural, and legal conventions used throughout the Annotated Grant Agreement to distinguish MGA legal text from legal commentary, examples, and program-specific rules[cite: 2].

## Key Conventions
- **Grey Text Boxes:** Contain official MGA text with key concepts bolded and underlined[cite: 2].
- **Color Coding:**
  - **Green:** Practical examples[cite: 2].
  - **Red:** Structured lists[cite: 2].
  - **Orange:** Specific cases and exceptions[cite: 2].
  - **Purple:** Programme-specific rules[cite: 2].
- **Versioning:** Maintained as a living corporate document with version history and hyperlinks[cite: 2].
