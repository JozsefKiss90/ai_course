# Fill-in guide: Planning Workbook for Horizon Europe lump sum

Funding Profile he-lump-sum 1.0. Workbook: `planning-workbook.xlsx`.

## Before you start

- Type only into light-yellow cells (Human Inputs). White cells are formulas: do not overwrite them.
- The workbook is never submitted. You copy its Transfer Views into the Official Budget Table (Horizon Europe detailed lump sum budget table, v3.6) by hand.
- Lists that are Excel Tables grow: add a row inside the Table (Tab in its last cell, or Insert > Table Rows), never below it.
- The Checks panel on the Cover shows OK / warning / error per guard while you fill in.
- The workbook needs Excel 2021 or Microsoft 365 and contains no macros.

## Fill-in order

1. BE list: names, acronyms, countries and funding rates
2. WP list: work package names
3. Task List: task titles
4. Rate Table: personnel rates per Beneficiary/AE
5. Staff effort: effort per work package (or task)
6. Input Guards: the lead of each work package
7. Travel planner: trips
8. Equipment depreciation: equipment items
9. Itemised list: other cost items
10. Profile Constants: plausibility thresholds, if you want the warnings (optional)
11. Transfer View sheets: copy into the Submission Budget
12. Check sheets: compare with the Submission Budget's own summaries
13. Cover: review the Checks panel

## Sheet by sheet

### Cover

- **What to enter:** Nothing. Every cell is a formula or fixed data.
- **Unit:** -
- **Source:** Generated by the build.
- Shows the provenance (profile version, build date, Shape Parameters), the Checks panel, the fill-in order and the colour legend. Review the Checks panel last.

### BE list

- **What to enter:** For every Beneficiary and Affiliated Entity row: name, acronym, country (dropdown) and funding rate (dropdown).
- **Unit:** Text; the funding rate as a percentage.
- **Source:** The consortium's own details, as they will appear in the Submission Budget.
- The layout is the same as the `BE list` of the Official Budget Table (Horizon Europe detailed lump sum budget table, v3.6), so the same block can be pasted into both.
- A late Beneficiary or AE: add a row to the Table (an AE directly under its Beneficiary), then claim a spare block by typing its BE/AE nr in the first free spare row of the `Rate Table`.

### WP list

- **What to enter:** The name of every work package.
- **Unit:** Text.
- **Source:** The proposal's work plan.
- The layout is the same as the `WP list` of the Official Budget Table (Horizon Europe detailed lump sum budget table, v3.6).

### Task List

- **What to enter:** The title of every task.
- **Unit:** Text.
- **Source:** The proposal's work plan.
- A task row added to the Table gets no staff effort rows until the workbook is rebuilt.

### Input Guards

- **What to enter:** The lead of each work package: a BE/AE nr from the dropdown.
- **Unit:** A BE/AE nr.
- **Source:** The proposal's work plan.
- The flags below the leads (effort without a rate) are formulas: read them, do not type.

### Profile Constants

- **What to enter:** Optional plausibility thresholds: Personnel rate above (EUR per person-month); Person-months of one BE/AE in one work package above (person-months); Cost of one trip above (EUR). Leave a threshold blank to switch its warning off.
- **Unit:** As labelled beside each threshold.
- **Source:** Your own judgement of what is implausible for this proposal; there are no defaults.
- The Profile Constants above the thresholds are the Funding Profile's fixed conventions, each with its source. Formulas reference them; do not change them.

### CountryList

- **What to enter:** Nothing. Every cell is a formula or fixed data.
- **Unit:** -
- **Source:** The country list of the Official Budget Table (Horizon Europe detailed lump sum budget table, v3.6), verbatim.
- Feeds the Country dropdown of the `BE list`.

### Rate Table

- **What to enter:** A personnel rate for every Beneficiary/AE row and Staff Category (Senior scientists, Junior scientists, Technical personnel, Administrative personnel, Others (A.1), Natural persons under direct contract, Seconded persons, SME owners and natural person beneficiaries). In a spare row, first the BE/AE nr of the late partner that claims it.
- **Unit:** EUR per person-month
- **Source:** Each Beneficiary's own personnel costs, by its usual cost accounting practices.

### Staff effort

- **What to enter:** Effort per task, Beneficiary/AE and Staff Category.
- **Unit:** person-months
- **Source:** The proposal's work plan. The cost columns multiply the effort by the Rate Table: do not type them.

### Travel planner

- **What to enter:** One row per trip: BE/AE nr, WP, Travellers, One-way distance (km), Nights, Days, Destination country.
- **Unit:** As in each column header; distances are one-way, in km.
- **Source:** The planned trips. Prices are looked up by formula from: 5.1 Amounts for return air and combined air/rail journey (C(2024)5405 of 2024-07-31); Accommodation and subsistence unit costs (calls with opening date as from 13 May 2025) (EU Grants: Additional Information on Unit Costs and Contributions — Annex 2a and 2b, V1.11 – 01.05.2026).
- Distances under 400 km have no unit cost and show #N/A.
- The price columns are calculated: a trip row added to the Table is priced too.

### Travel summary

- **What to enter:** Nothing. Every cell is a formula or fixed data.
- **Unit:** -
- **Source:** Sums the `Travel planner` per Beneficiary/AE and work package.

### Travel unit costs

- **What to enter:** Nothing. Every cell is a formula or fixed data.
- **Unit:** EUR
- **Source:** 5.1 Amounts for return air and combined air/rail journey (C(2024)5405 of 2024-07-31); Accommodation and subsistence unit costs (calls with opening date as from 13 May 2025) (EU Grants: Additional Information on Unit Costs and Contributions — Annex 2a and 2b, V1.11 – 01.05.2026)

### Equipment depreciation

- **What to enter:** One row per item: BE/AE nr, WP, Cost line, Item, Purchase cost (EUR), Depreciation period (months), Usage share for the action.
- **Unit:** As in each column header; the usage share as a percentage.
- **Source:** The planned purchases, with the depreciation period from the Beneficiary's own accounting.
- Only the depreciation that falls within the project is costed, assuming purchase in project month 1.

### Equipment summary

- **What to enter:** Nothing. Every cell is a formula or fixed data.
- **Unit:** -
- **Source:** Sums the `Equipment depreciation` per Beneficiary/AE, work package and cost line.

### Itemised list

- **What to enter:** One row per item: BE/AE nr, WP, Cost line, Item, Number of items, Cost per item (EUR).
- **Unit:** As in each column header.
- **Source:** Quotes, price lists or past costs for each item.
- The cost line is picked from the list beside the Table: B. DIRECT SUBCONTRACTING COSTS; Consumables; Services for meetings, seminars; Services for dissemination activities (including website); Publication fees; Other (shipment, insurance, translation, etc.); D.1 Financial support to third parties (if applicable in the topic specific conditions); D.2 Internally invoiced goods and services; D.3 Transnational access to research infrastructure unit costs (if mentioned as eligible in the topic specific conditions); D.4 Virtual access to research infrastructure unit costs (if mentioned as eligible in the topic specific conditions); D.5 PCP/PPI procurement costs (if mentioned as eligible in the topic specific conditions).

### Itemised list summary

- **What to enter:** Nothing. Every cell is a formula or fixed data.
- **Unit:** -
- **Source:** Sums the `Itemised list` per Beneficiary/AE, work package and cost line.

### Transfer View sheets

- **What to enter:** Nothing. Every cell is a formula or fixed data.
- **Unit:** As in the Submission Budget: number of items, average cost per item, total (EUR).
- **Source:** Every Estimation Method contributing to a cost line, added up.
- One sheet per Beneficiary and AE, plus 0 spare block(s) for late partners. Copy them into the Official Budget Table (Horizon Europe detailed lump sum budget table, v3.6) as described at the end of this guide.

### Check sheets

- **What to enter:** Nothing. Every cell is a formula or fixed data.
- **Unit:** EUR, person-months and percentage shares.
- **Source:** The Transfer Views, summarised the way the Official Budget Table (Horizon Europe detailed lump sum budget table, v3.6) summarises them.
- Compare them with the Submission Budget's own summaries after the transfer.

## Transferring the Transfer Views into the Official Budget Table (Horizon Europe detailed lump sum budget table, v3.6)

1. In the Official Budget Table, fill its `BE list` and `WP list`: copy the data block of the Planning Workbook's `BE list` and `WP list` and paste it into the same cells there (Paste Special > Values). The layouts are identical.
2. Let the Official Budget Table generate its budget sheets with its own buttons: one sheet per Beneficiary (BE1, BE2, ...), with a column block for each of its Affiliated Entities.
3. For each `Transfer View BEn` sheet: on the Official Budget Table's sheet of that Beneficiary, work package by work package and line by line, type ITEMS and COST PER ITEM into the BE columns. The rows are in the same order.
4. For each `Transfer View BEn-AEm` sheet: do the same in that Affiliated Entity's column block (ITEMS, COST PER ITEM) on its Beneficiary's sheet.
5. A spare block (`Transfer View spare k`) is transferred like the Beneficiary or Affiliated Entity whose code was typed into its Rate Table row; an unused spare block is not transferred.
6. Transfer values only, never formulas. Leave a line blank where the Transfer View shows no items.
7. Do not type totals, indirect costs (E) or summary rows: the Official Budget Table calculates them. Compare them with the Transfer View instead.
8. Finally, compare the Official Budget Table's Lump sum breakdown, Person-months overview and Summary per WP with the Planning Workbook's `Check - ...` sheets; every figure should match.
